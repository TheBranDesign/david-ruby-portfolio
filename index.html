A career in constructability

Engineering knowledge meets the realities of construction.

David I. Ruby, PE, SE, FASCE, has spent a career working at the intersection of structural engineering, fabrication, and field execution. From early bridge erection calculations to some of the country's most recognizable buildings and stadiums, his work has focused on making designs practical, efficient, and buildable.

After founding Ruby and Associates in 1984 and leading the firm for more than 39 years, David retired following its acquisition by Degenkolb. He now occasionally provides constructability consulting to the structural steel industry when requested.

Sharing knowledge

Putting construction knowledge into the design process.

David authored AISC Design Guide #23, Constructability of Structural Steel Buildings, published in 2008. The guide draws on the Construction Industry Institute's definition of constructability: the optimal use of construction knowledge and experience in planning, design, procurement, and field operations to achieve overall project objectives.

Its principles emphasize integrated teams, construction knowledge, communication, available resources, site constraints, innovation, and shared feedback.
